# Privacy Policy — Draft

This is a technical placeholder and must be reviewed and completed by the app owner before public launch.

The service is designed to collect only the information needed to operate a voting-mission account: nickname, email address, broad voting region (Australia or international), mission history and evidence voluntarily submitted for verification.

Users should crop or cover unnecessary personal information before uploading voting confirmations. Proof files are intended to remain private and accessible only to the submitting user and authorised moderators. The service does not request passwords or credentials for external voting platforms.

Before launch, specify the operator identity/contact details, lawful basis/consent model, retention periods, deletion request process, data processors (including Supabase/GitHub where applicable), international transfers, age requirements and applicable privacy rights.
